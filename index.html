Questions before
you go solar.

Do you install solar for homes and factories in Bhiwadi?+

Yes. We provide residential, commercial and industrial solar installation in Bhiwadi and nearby areas. Share your site location and electricity use so we can discuss your requirements.

Do you serve Tapukara, Khushkhera and Chopanki?+

We welcome enquiries from these areas, as well as Tijara, Kotkasim and nearby villages. Please share your exact location to confirm a site visit.

How much does rooftop solar installation cost?+

The quotation depends on capacity, equipment, roof structure, access and connection requirements. Send a recent electricity bill and property details to start a discussion.

What size solar system will my home need?+

We assess your electricity use, usable roof area, shading and connection details before proposing a capacity. A neighbour’s system size may not suit your property.

Can I apply for PM Surya Ghar?+

Eligibility depends on the residential electricity connection and the scheme requirements. We can discuss the application steps. Check the official PM Surya Ghar portal for current requirements.

Where is your office?+

Our headquarters is in Alwar, with a regional office serving Behror–Neemrana. Our Bhiwadi city office is coming soon. Contact our Bhiwadi enquiry team by phone, WhatsApp or email.
